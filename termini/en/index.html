2. What the service is

Tutora reads the barcode of dog and cat food and shows a score from 0 to 100 calculated from the label: composition, analytical constituents and additives. How the score works is explained on the Scoring method page.

It also shows product recall notices published by official sources.

3. The score is not veterinary advice

The score is indicative information, based on what the label declares and on public sources. It is not veterinary advice, a diagnosis or a prescription.

For your pet's diet, for allergies, intolerances and diseases, always ask your veterinarian. Veterinary dietetic foods do not get a score.

The score assesses the product in general, not whether it suits a specific animal. We do not know additive quantities, raw material quality or digestibility, which are not on the label. Data may contain errors, for example if the label has changed or was misread. If you find an error, write to us or send an updated photo from the app.

Personal notes (for example "contains chicken, which you listed among the sensitivities") only compare the words on the label with what you entered. No note does not mean the product is suitable for your pet.

4. Recalls

Recall notices come from official sources, such as the Italian Ministry of Health and the recall pages of retail chains. We check them before publishing. They may still arrive late, be incomplete or not concern your batch.

The official notice always prevails; it is linked in every recall. Not receiving an alert does not mean a product has not been recalled. If in doubt, check the batch on the package and ask the shop or the manufacturer.
